# Product Requirements: Lucas Abreu - Automation & AI Portfolio
**Project ID:** lucas-abreu56/portifolio_dev

## 1. Product Overview
A high-end personal portfolio and landing page designed to showcase the professional services, engineering expertise, and completed projects of Lucas Abreu, specializing in Intelligent Automation, AI Agents, and Web/Interface Development.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Enterprise Clients & Businesses:** Looking to streamline their operations, build automated workflows, or integrate custom AI assistants.
- **Tech Recruiters & Talent Acquisition:** Assessing engineering skills, project complexity, and technical portfolio depth.
- **Collaborators & Partners:** Looking to co-develop or integrate automation pipelines.

## 3. Key Features & Capabilities
- **Bilingual Interface (i18n):** Clean language toggling between English (EN) and Portuguese (PT-BR) with state persistence using the browser's `localStorage` to cater to both local and international audiences.
- **Interactive Project Showcase:** Categorized into:
  - *Automation & AI Agents:* Highlighting complex system integrations (e.g., N8N workflow engines, custom Telegram bots, RAG pipelines, database integrations).
  - *Websites & Interfaces:* Demonstrating high-fidelity frontend layouts, web apps, and interactive landings.
- **Bento Grid Services:** A clean structured list detailing main offerings:
  - *Intelligent Automation:* Complex workflows with N8N and Python.
  - *Agent Engineering:* RAG pipelines and custom AI assistants.
  - *Specialized Prompting:* Prompt engineering and structuring.
- **Micro-Interactions & Transitions:** Fluid animations (staggered card reveal, laser sweeps, shimmer buttons) designed to delight users and reflect the "high-tech" branding of the services.
- **SEO & Performance Optimization:** Clean Server-Side Rendering (SSR) pages delivering fast LCP and optimized accessibility.

## 4. User Flow & Navigation
1. **Landing / Hero Fold:** Users land on the page, greeting them with the main title and introducing them to the core value proposition. They can instantly toggle language or scroll down.
2. **Projects Gallery:** Users explore projects horizontally. Hovering highlights the card's active tech stack, and clicking redirects to live apps or active bots (e.g., Telegram bots).
3. **Services Breakdown:** Explores how the developer can help their businesses.
4. **Contact & Socials:** Quick access links to external profiles (LinkedIn, GitHub, Gmail) to initiate business communication.
